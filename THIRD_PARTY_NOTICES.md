## Installed into the build tree, not linked

With plotting enabled, the first configure creates `build/pyenv` and installs
**matplotlib** and its dependencies into it. These are separate programs invoked
through the embedded interpreter, not linked into the binary, and are not
redistributed unless that directory is shipped alongside it. Every one is
permissive: matplotlib under its own Python Software Foundation-style agreement;
numpy, contourpy, kiwisolver, cycler and python-dateutil under BSD; fonttools,
pyparsing, six and pillow under MIT or MIT-CMU; packaging under Apache-2.0 or
BSD-2-Clause. Their full texts ship inside `build/pyenv` with the packages
themselves.
