# Atmosphere, the propagation seam, and profiles (`--atmosphere`, `--wind`)

The user-facing description is in the manual's
[Broadband and spectral paths](../MANUAL.md#broadband-and-spectral-paths),
[Stratified atmosphere](../MANUAL.md#stratified-atmosphere) and
[A profile from the surface wind](../MANUAL.md#a-profile-from-the-surface-wind). The equations are
in [THEORY.md](../THEORY.md) §4 and §7, and the measured scores in
[FIDELITY.md](../FIDELITY.md#wind-profile-and-stability).

## ISO 9613-1 absorption

`src/atmosphere.{hpp,cpp}` is ISO 9613-1:1993 atmospheric absorption, ported term for term from
panam's `panam_acoustics/iso_9613_1_1993.py` and pinned against values generated by running that
module (`tests/test_atmosphere.cpp`). The A-weighting the spectral path sums with is panam's
`dBAw`, pinned in `tests/test_weighting.cpp` (see [ground.md](ground.md#weighting)). A change to
either is a change to panam first.

- **Pressure is in kPa**, matching the standard and the Python. It is the one non-SI unit in the
  codebase.
- **`absorption_coefficients()` is the form the hot path uses.** It hoists the two relaxation
  frequencies, the classical loss, the oxygen and nitrogen exponential numerators, and the
  temperature power out of the per-band loop. It preserves the arithmetic order and is bit-exact
  against the per-frequency call and the original formula over a temperature, pressure and
  humidity sweep.

## The propagation seam (`RayPath`)

`src/propagation.hpp` is the propagation seam. `straight_ray()` returns a `RayPath`: the direction
the sphere is looked up by, the range that spreading and absorption take, and the ground model's
source height and ground distance. `spectral_level_at_observer` takes all of these from it and
nothing from raw geometry. The seam went in bit-identical on every path. A refracting model
returns the same record per eigenray.

The header lists what such a model must *also* change, which deliberately does not live there:

- reception time (`reception_factor` differentiates straight-line range);
- the bounds (they subtract straight-line spreading; focusing adds gain);
- the broadband path, which shares its geometry with the bound test and does not use the record.

## Stratified profiles (`--atmosphere`)

`src/atmosphere_profile.{hpp,cpp}` holds a stratified T/RH/p profile and `LayeredAbsorption`,
per-band absorption integrated through it. Along a straight path a band loses the coefficient's
mean over the heights spanned times the path length. The mean is
(F_b(z_s) − F_b(z_o)) / (z_s − z_o), with F a 1 m table of α's running integral. The things to
preserve:

- **Profile fields share one height lookup.** Temperature, humidity and pressure use one cell in
  `at()`, and the two wind components use one in `wind_at()`. Endpoints are held without reading
  their neighbors, and pressure keeps its geometric interpolation.
- **Hydrostatic pressure is built in linear time.** Successive levels reuse the integral through
  complete earlier layers, keeping their original left-to-right addition order. The final layer
  keeps the old endpoint interpolation separately from the prefix, so its rounding does not
  change. Tests compare exactly with independent walks for uneven layers, nearly isothermal
  segments, and ground below, within or above the profile.
- **The hot loop pays nothing on a grid, and a subtract and a multiply per band otherwise.** F at
  each state's height is looked up once per trajectory. When every observer shares a height, as on
  any grid, each state's mean is formed once too (`state_absorption_mean`), and the loop only reads
  it. Without that the profile cost about 5% of the acoustic loop. Scattered observers at mixed
  heights take the per-pair path; `atmosphere_uniform_scattered_is_homogeneous` runs
  `arrays/coyle_approach.csv` through it.
- **`Database` knows nothing about profiles.** The caller hands `spectral_level_at_observer`
  per-band coefficients exactly as before. Without `--atmosphere` the run-wide array goes through
  untouched, so the default output is byte-identical.
- **F is quadratic between nodes.** α is linear between nodes and F is its exact integral, so the
  difference quotient tends smoothly to α as the heights meet. Within 1 mm it switches to α at the
  midpoint (`mean_coefficients`).
- **One level means uniform at every height.** With several levels, a trajectory above the top is
  refused, not extrapolated.
- **The surface level supplies the sound speed** for the reception time and the ground impedance.
- **Spectral path only.** `EAA` is a fitted broadband attenuation with nothing per band to
  integrate.
- **`atmosphere_uniform_is_homogeneous` compares at 1e-9, not byte for byte.** A mean of a uniform
  integral is the coefficient to a few ULP. Do not "fix" it by comparing bytes.
- **Wind columns are ignored** by the absorption and named in the log.

## The surface-wind profile (`--wind`)

`--wind` builds the profile instead of reading one (`synthetic_profile`, `SurfaceWind`): a neutral
log law through one surface observation, held above `--surface_layer_depth`, a dry-adiabatic lapse
from the run's surface air, hydrostatic pressure, and water vapor held. `--stability` swaps the log
law for a power law and the lapse for a class gradient in the surface layer. The things to
preserve:

- **Never emit a calm level above z = 0.** `--refraction stratified` takes one (rows at 0 and
  0.1 ft, both still) as a thin layer that put levels 22–28 dB low. The log law is zero at z₀, so
  the first level above the ground is ten roughness lengths up (or the anemometer, if lower:
  `SurfaceWind::first_level()`), and `synthetic_profile` throws `logic_error` if a level there
  comes out calm. Do not "refine near the ground" by adding levels at or below z₀. A calm
  observation gives a profile with *no wind columns*, not columns of zeros;
  `refer_to_database_speed` treats a `--wind` of zero as calm stated, so `--speed_reference air` is
  not refused for it.
- **Each trajectory's air travels in its own copy of the run's options** (`--atmosphere`, `--wind`,
  `--humidity`, `--temperature`, `--pressure`, `--start_utc`, once for the run or once each).
  `run()` fills that copy beside the trajectory's `PathJob` and hands it to `process_trajectory`, so
  everything below it that reads `options` sees that trajectory's air without knowing there are
  several. The run's options hold the first trajectory's. Each distinct `--wind` and `--atmosphere`
  is parsed once. `air_*_is_its_own` and `air_clock_is_each_tracks_own` pin each of the six against
  a run alone.
- **Built per trajectory, in `process_trajectory`, not in `run()`.** Its surface air is that
  trajectory's vehicle file's (under `--temperature` and friends, which `--wind`, unlike
  `--atmosphere`, accepts), and its top has to clear that track. Inside `process_trajectory` the
  profile is the local `atmosphere`, never `options.atmosphere`, which is empty under `--wind`.
  Anything new that reads the profile there has to take the local.
- **The top is max(surface layer, 1.05 × highest path), and no lower.** Above both, the wind is
  held and the air lapses, so c + w·h falls with height in every direction and no ray returns from
  air the profile leaves out. A stable class's inversion runs to the top of the surface layer,
  which is why the top never stops at the trajectory.
- **The anemometer, the top of the surface layer and the top are exact levels.** The profile
  passes through the observation, and the kink at the layer's top is a level rather than smeared
  into whichever layer straddles it.
- **The bottom layer, 0 to 10 z₀, is linear, and `--profile_levels` does not refine it.** Filling
  it with the log law (fed through `--atmosphere`), or dropping every level below 1 m, barely
  moves the day-284 results ([FIDELITY](../FIDELITY.md#wind-profile-and-stability)). Do not
  "fix" it by starting the levels nearer z₀: those levels carry almost no wind, which is the thin
  layer the stratified model cannot take.
- **The stability classes are unvalidated, and warn.** They are TA Luft's exponents and RG 1.23's
  gradients, as SELENE has them. One day scored after the fact chooses nothing
  ([FIDELITY](../FIDELITY.md#wind-profile-and-stability)). Do not make a class a default without
  measurements of the class.

## The profile builder (`niceops_build_atmosphere`)

`src/atmosphere_builder.{hpp,cpp}` builds an `--atmosphere` profile from measured columns
(`read_column_source`, `build_profile`). Its front end is `tools/build_atmosphere.cpp`. The
equations are in [THEORY.md](../THEORY.md) §7. The things to preserve:

- **It writes through `write_atmosphere_profile` and reads its output back through
  `read_atmosphere_profile`**, so anything the run would refuse is refused when the profile is
  built, and the file replays to the bit.
- **The fill below the lowest wind level is `SurfaceWind`'s law, not a copy.** A `SurfaceWind` is
  built on that level and `speed_at` evaluated. When the lowest level is the station's
  (`--wind`), the law takes the station's own speed and bearing, so a build from one
  observation is `synthetic_profile` to the bit. The Catch2 case compares every double with `==`
  over the classes, roughness lengths and anemometers below and above 10 z₀. Arithmetic that
  departs from `synthetic_profile`'s order fails it, including the temperature's spelling
  (`T0 + Γ min(z, δ) + lapse max(z − δ, 0)`) and the `+ 0.0` on the wind.
- **`geometric_levels` is shared with `synthetic_profile`**, so a change there moves both.
  `find_profile_columns` is shared with `read_atmosphere_profile`, so the two accept the same
  headers. The tool's reader adds only the dew point and specific humidity.
- **No fill level in (0, first level).** The geometric levels start at min(10 z₀, z_g), for the
  same reason as `--wind`'s; measured levels below that stay levels. A temperature, humidity or
  pressure measured at or below z₀ under the log law (a screen-height sensor at a forest or urban
  site) is left out of the levels with a warning, not refused: its value still enters the
  interpolation. An explicit `--levels` height there would be calm and is refused. A measured
  calm level is refused, or dropped with `--calm_levels drop`. A column calm at every level gives
  a profile without wind columns.
- **The explicit level list exists because the stratified search follows the layers.** Grid cells
  1500 m downwind move ±0.2 dB under any re-layering (see the discretization bullet below), so
  reproducing a profile someone else ran needs their levels, not only their values.
- **z₀ has no default.** Nothing measures it, and a factor of 3 either way spans −6 to +6 dB in
  the PE on the validation harness's unstable paths (harness `paper/notes/surface_layer.md`,
  section 4), so a profile never carries a silent one.
- **No Monin–Obukhov fill.** It failed the harness's do-no-harm gate on the R44 approaches
  (rms +0.44 [+0.14, +0.71] dB against the log law) and on day 284's mean window (same note,
  section 3). Keep it out unless a new fill passes that gate first.
- **Pressure outside its measurements is hydrostatic from the end measurement**, through
  `with_hydrostatic_pressure`'s ratios at the profile's levels. A measured level is a level, so
  the end knot has an exact ratio, unless it was left out at or below z₀; there the ratio is
  log-linear between the levels about it. Pressure from a surface value alone is
  `with_hydrostatic_pressure` itself.
- **The roughness-length fit solves dS/dℓ = 0 by bisection**, after a scan in ln z₀ brackets the
  minimum, so exact log-law runs give z₀ back to 1e-9. A minimizer's tolerance (Brent's
  √ε) would not. The bootstrap draws its indices with `portable_index`, rejection on
  `mt19937_64`'s own bits. `std::uniform_int_distribution` is implementation-defined, and the
  interval would differ between libstdc++ and libc++. A Catch2 case pins a sequence.
- **The percentile interval undercovers slightly.** Over 100 seeds of 20 runs with 1% speed noise
  it covers the true z₀ in 90 (the hidden `[.slow]` case, at its floor). Do not tighten that case
  without changing the interval's method.

## Wind-profile tests

- **The law, the air and the conventions**, in `tests/test_atmosphere_profile.cpp`: the profile
  passes through the observation and is zero at z₀; the temperature, pressure and molar
  concentration against their closed forms; a wind from 270° blows toward the east and equals what
  `--atmosphere` makes of the same wind written as speed and direction; the speed's units; no calm
  level above the ground for any class, roughness or anemometer height; and the written profile
  reads back to the bit (heights to an ULP of the unit conversion).
- **`--write_atmosphere` replays to 1e-9** (`wind_is_its_written_profile`): 17 digits, the wind as
  u/v components rather than speed and direction (no trigonometric round trip), and the pressure
  written, so the replay does not re-integrate it.
- **A replay compares the run with itself**, so it is blind to the plumbing in `run()` and
  `wind_profile`. `wind_profile_written` runs in feet and sets every shaping option and the three
  surface overrides; `wind_profile_is_its_law` holds the profile it writes to an independent
  closed-form oracle (`tests/data/wind_profile_expected.{py,csv}`). The two agree to 1e-12
  absolute at every level; the test compares at `golden_compare`'s 1e-9. It fails on a length
  left in feet, a dropped `--temperature`/`--pressure` override, or `--profile_levels` not passed
  on. Change the generator on purpose and regenerate the CSV from the script, not from the
  binary.
- **Discretization is checked through the real run** (`wind_is_discretization_independent`: 16 →
  64 levels a decade, plate microphones over the Coyle array under stratified refraction, 0.016 dB
  of a 0.1 dB allowance; 0.053 dB from 8 levels). Its effect on the measured day-284 runs is in
  [FIDELITY](../FIDELITY.md#wind-profile-and-stability). The test is on plates and not on a
  point-receiver grid for a reason: grid cells 1500 m out downwind move ±0.2 dB with *any* change of
  layering and do not settle from 8 levels a decade to 100. That is the stratified arrival search,
  whose samples follow the layers, not the profile. Do not tighten the tolerance onto that floor,
  or chase it here.
- **The builder** is pinned in `tests/test_atmosphere_builder.cpp`: one observation is
  `synthetic_profile` with `==`; the fill is the law through the lowest gate; no level above the
  ground is calm for random gates and roughness lengths, nor for a temperature and pressure measured
  below z₀ (left out as levels, and still setting the ground's values); ground levels from the
  station, across the ground, or held; the explicit level list; the column reader's wind convention
  against `read_atmosphere_profile`, the dew point and specific humidity against closed forms, and
  fill values; and a built profile reads back as itself.
